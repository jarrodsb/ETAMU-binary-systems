## Results

- When I define a Rebound simulation similar to that of the Alpha Centauri system, but with a 1-Earth-mass planet at 2 AU and a 2-Earth-mass planet at 3 AU from the primary star, my classifier returns a stability probability of 0.63. When I change the 2nd planet from 3 AU to 4 AU, I get a probability value of 0.60. These are both fairly high probabilities, which is expected for a real-world analogous system with known stability. It also demonstrates that my classifier gives different results for different inputs.
