## Results

- $\sin{\lambda_p}$ and $\cos{\lambda_p}$ are used to map the initial orbital mean longitude position onto a continuous 2D Euclidean unit circle, because standard decision trees cannot handle wrap-around boundaries and would treat 1∘ and 359∘ as opposite extremes of the feature space.
- The version of Model C that performed the best was a configuration that featured shallower trees (`max_depth = 4`) and a more conservative learning rate (`learning_rate = 0.01`) compared to the SPOCK configuration of (`max_depth = 5`) and (`learning_rate = 0.05`).
- The hyperparameter experiment shows that SPOCK’s hyperparameter configuration does not transfer out of the box to hierarchical stellar binaries.
