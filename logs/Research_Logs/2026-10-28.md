## Results

- The optimal hyperparameter configuration is Aggressive Learning Rate (`n_estimators = 250`, `max_depth = 5`, `learning_rate = 0.15`), with an AUC score of 0.9943.
- The highest importance features were `emax_short` (40.1%) and `delta_e_short` (23.4%), both short-term dynamics-related features.
- The $A_c$ / $A_{bin}$ prediction parity plots show an $R^2$ value of 0.8602 for Model B and 0.8148 for Model C, with most residuals in the negative region.
- The Quarles et al. 2020 Figure 7 recreation plots yielded a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) regression metric of around 0.03 or less for all sub-panels, for both Models B and C.

## Problems or questions

- Classifier models B and C both appear to struggle with predicting $A_c$ / $A_{bin}$ for highly inclined orbital configurations.
